Delulu is the solulu

A two-player card game about how well you actually know each other.

Played in your browser with a regular deck of cards. Answer weird questions with numbers, try to match. ~15 minutes.

▶ Play now — it's free How it works

Someone reads a question out loud — like “How scary is a spider?” Both players secretly pick a number from 1 to 10 and lay a card face down. Flip together. Match or come close, you earn chips together. Way off, you earn nothing — but you learn how differently you each see the world.

It’s cooperative, not competitive. No trivia, no right answers. Just: can you think like each other? It starts easy, gets wonderfully strange, and ends with a moment of pure intuition.

How to play

Open the game on any phone or laptop — it reads out the prompts and keeps score. You play along with a real deck of cards.

1
Split a deck: Ace–10 of two suits (Ace = 1, no face cards). Each player takes one suit. Grab ~50 chips — coins, candy, pasta, anything.
2
The game shows a prompt. Both players secretly commit one card face down.
3
Flip at the same time, tap how close you matched, and the game scores it. Repeat for 10 rounds.
